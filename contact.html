Common Questions

Before you get in touch

How quickly will you reply?

Within one working day. If you send a recent electricity bill and a photo of your roof with your enquiry, that first reply can usually include a realistic ballpark figure rather than just a request for more information.

Do you charge for a site survey?

No. The site survey is free and comes with no obligation. We check your roof structure, orientation, shading, consumer unit and meter, then design around what we actually find — and if the numbers don't work for you, we'll say so.

Which areas do you cover?

We work right across Phuket, Phang Nga and Krabi from our office in Kathu. For projects further afield, our nationwide partner installer network means we can cover the rest of Thailand too.
