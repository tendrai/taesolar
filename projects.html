Anuban Trang School

A 100 kW rooftop system across the school's main buildings, with the inverters and distribution boards housed in a dedicated plant room. Teaching hours line up almost exactly with solar generation, which is what makes schools such strong candidates for self-consumption.

100 kWTrangSchool rooftop

Nurse University, Trang

The largest system in this set: a 250 kW array spread over several rooftops on the college campus, feeding buildings with steady weekday daytime demand. Multiple string inverters sit in a shared plant room with the DC and AC distribution.

250 kWTrangCommercial rooftop

Mae Suai Dam, off-grid

A ground-mounted array beside a farmhouse without a reliable grid connection, paired with an inverter and battery system installed inside the house. Off-grid design is about matching storage to the evening load, not just fitting panels.

10 kW / 32 kWMae SuaiOff-grid ground mount

Patrol boat, Songkhla Lake

Panels mounted on the roof of a lake patrol boat, with a battery bank and charge controllers fitted below so the boat can run its electrics without a generator. Marine work means corrosion-resistant fixings and cabling rated for constant vibration and spray.

5 kW / 20 kWSongkhlaMarine off-grid

Private residence, Kathu, Phuket

A rooftop system on a family home a few minutes from our office. Concrete tile roofs like this one need careful waterproofing at every fixing, and coastal Phuket calls for corrosion-resistant mounting hardware throughout.
