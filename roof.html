Measure your roof, garden or land

Trace your roof, garden or plot of land on the satellite view and we'll tell you how much solar it can physically carry — then feed that straight into the savings calculator.

  1. 1

    Find your property on the map. Use the location button, or pinch and drag to it.

  2. 2

    Tap each corner in turn to trace the outline. Drag a point to adjust it, or tap a point to remove it.

  3. 3

    Read your maximum system size, then scroll down for the savings that size would produce.
